How it works: Events are partitioned into time buckets. Keys that co-occur in the same bucket are linked by Jaccard similarity; pairs above the threshold are merged into clusters. When a failure key is given, co-occurring keys are ranked by how consistently they fire before the failure — positive lead time means a probable root cause, negative means a downstream consequence.
