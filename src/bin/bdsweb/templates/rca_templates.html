How it works: Each drain3 template observation is treated as an event (template_body, log_timestamp). Bodies that fire in the same time bucket are linked by Jaccard similarity; pairs above the threshold are merged into clusters. When a failure body is given, co-occurring templates are ranked by how consistently they fire before the failure — positive lead time means a probable root cause, negative means a downstream consequence.
