Projecting… Analyzing…
How it works: Primary records in the training window are bucketed into states via drain3 templates, a higher-order Markov chain is fit over the state sequence, and inter-arrival times are learned from the observed gaps. The chain is rolled out N samples times forward; the modal state per time bin (with its share-of-samples consensus) is returned as the projection. High-consensus events (≥ 0.66) are what the chain agreed on across most rollouts; lower consensus (~0.33) means partial agreement; the rest is exploratory.
